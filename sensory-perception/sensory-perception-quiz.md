## Question 1

<p>A nurse is preparing to assess a client's distance visual acuity using a Snellen chart. Which action should the nurse take?</p>

<p class="answer-choice"><strong>A.</strong> Position the client 20 feet from the chart while wearing prescribed distance lenses.</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>B.</strong> Position the client 10 feet from the chart without corrective lenses.</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>C.</strong> Position the client 14 inches from the chart while covering both eyes.</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>D.</strong> Position the client 40 feet from the chart while wearing reading glasses.</p>

<details class="quiz-answer-block">
<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: A</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A. Correct:</strong> The lecture specifies testing at 20 feet and having the client wear prescribed distance correction.</p>
<p><strong>B. Incorrect:</strong> Option B uses the wrong distance and removes needed correction.</p>
<p><strong>C. Incorrect:</strong> Option C describes neither distance testing nor a valid eye assessment.</p>
<p><strong>D. Incorrect:</strong> Option D uses an incorrect distance and the wrong type of lenses.</p>
</div>
</details>

<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A. Incorrect:</strong> Option B describes loss of near focusing with age rather than this acuity result.</p>
