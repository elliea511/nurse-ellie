## Question 1

<p>A nurse is assessing a client who has primary open-angle glaucoma. Which finding should the nurse expect?</p>

<p class="answer-choice"><strong>A.</strong> Gradual loss of peripheral vision</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>B.</strong> Sudden severe eye pain</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>C.</strong> Fixed mid-dilated pupil</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>D.</strong> Rapid onset of blurred central vision</p>

<details class="quiz-answer-block">
<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: A</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A. Correct:</strong> This disorder progresses slowly and commonly begins with peripheral visual-field loss.</p>
<p><strong>B. Incorrect:</strong> Sudden severe pain is more consistent with acute angle-closure glaucoma.</p>
<p><strong>C. Incorrect:</strong> A fixed mid-dilated pupil is associated with acute angle closure.</p>
<p><strong>D. Incorrect:</strong> Rapid central vision loss is not the typical early pattern.</p>
</div>
</details>

## Question 2

<p>A nurse is teaching a client newly diagnosed with primary open-angle glaucoma. Which statement is most important?</p>

<p class="answer-choice"><strong>A.</strong> “Treatment is needed only when eye pain occurs.”</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>B.</strong> “Eye drops must be used consistently to prevent vision loss.”</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>C.</strong> “Vision will return after pressure is controlled.”</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>D.</strong> “The disorder usually resolves without long-term therapy.”</p>

<details class="quiz-answer-block">
<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: B</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A. Incorrect:</strong> Open-angle glaucoma is often painless, so symptoms are not a reliable guide for treatment.</p>
<p><strong>B. Correct:</strong> Consistent therapy lowers intraocular pressure and helps prevent irreversible optic nerve damage.</p>
<p><strong>C. Incorrect:</strong> Existing optic nerve damage and lost vision are generally irreversible.</p>
<p><strong>D. Incorrect:</strong> This is a chronic condition requiring ongoing monitoring and treatment.</p>
</div>
</details>
