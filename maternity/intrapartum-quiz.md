## Question 1

<p>Which component of the birth process refers to uterine contractions and maternal pushing efforts?</p>

<p class="answer-choice"><strong>A.</strong> Powers</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>B.</strong> Passage</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>C.</strong> Passenger</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>D.</strong> Psyche</p>

<details class="quiz-answer-block">
<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: A</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A.</strong> Correct. Powers include involuntary uterine contractions and voluntary maternal pushing.</p>
<p><strong>B.</strong> Incorrect. Passage refers to the bony pelvis and soft tissues.</p>
<p><strong>C.</strong> Incorrect. Passenger refers to the fetus, placenta, membranes, and amniotic fluid.</p>
<p><strong>D.</strong> Incorrect. Psyche refers to the emotional and psychological response to labor.</p>
</div>
</details>

## Question 6

<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: A</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A.</strong> Correct. Frequency is measured from the beginning of one contraction to the beginning of the next.</p>
