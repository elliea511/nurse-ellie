## Question 1

<p>Which action is the priority immediately after birth for a vigorous term newborn?</p>

<p class="answer-choice"><strong>A.</strong> Dry the newborn thoroughly</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>B.</strong> Administer hepatitis B vaccine</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>C.</strong> Measure head circumference</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>D.</strong> Perform the first bath</p>

<details class="quiz-answer-block">
<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: A</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A.</strong> Correct. Drying reduces evaporative heat loss and supports thermoregulation.</p>
<p><strong>B.</strong> Incorrect. Immunization is important but not the first priority.</p>
<p><strong>C.</strong> Incorrect. Measurements can wait until the newborn is stable.</p>
<p><strong>D.</strong> Incorrect. Bathing is delayed until temperature is stable.</p>
</div>
</details>

<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
