## Question 1

<p>A nurse is teaching a female client how to obtain a clean-catch urine specimen. Which action should the client take?</p>

<p class="answer-choice"><strong>A.</strong> Separate the labia, cleanse from front to back, begin voiding, then collect midstream urine.</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>B.</strong> Cleanse after voiding, then pour urine from the collection hat into the sterile cup.</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>C.</strong> Cleanse from back to front, collect the first urine, then finish voiding into the toilet.</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>D.</strong> Keep the labia closed, begin voiding directly into the cup, then finish in the toilet.</p>

<details class="quiz-answer-block">
<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: A</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A. Correct:</strong> This technique reduces contamination and obtains the midstream specimen required for culture.</p>
<p><strong>B. Incorrect:</strong> This transfers urine from a nonsterile container and cleanses too late.</p>
<p><strong>C. Incorrect:</strong> This moves organisms toward the urethra and collects the contaminated initial stream.</p>
<p><strong>D. Incorrect:</strong> The labia should remain separated during cleansing and collection.</p>
</div>
</details>
