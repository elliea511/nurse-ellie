## Question 1

<p>A nurse is teaching a client how to collect a clean-catch urine specimen. Which action should the client take?</p>

<p class="answer-choice"><strong>A.</strong> Begin voiding, then collect urine midstream</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>B.</strong> Collect the initial urine stream</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>C.</strong> Touch the inside of the cup to the skin</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>D.</strong> Pour urine from a collection hat into the cup</p>

<details class="quiz-answer-block">
<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: A</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A. Correct:</strong> Midstream collection after cleansing reduces contamination.</p>
<p><strong>B. Incorrect:</strong> The initial stream can contain urethral organisms.</p>
<p><strong>C. Incorrect:</strong> Touching the cup contaminates it.</p>
<p><strong>D. Incorrect:</strong> A clean-catch specimen should be collected directly.</p>
</div>
</details>
