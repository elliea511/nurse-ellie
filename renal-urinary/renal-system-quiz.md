## Question 1

<p>A nurse is reviewing the record of a client who has an acute increase in serum creatinine after several days of vomiting and diarrhea. Which finding most strongly supports prerenal acute kidney injury?</p>

<p class="answer-choice"><strong>A.</strong> Urine sodium of 12 mEq/L</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>B.</strong> Urine sediment containing muddy brown casts</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>C.</strong> Bladder scan showing 650 mL of retained urine</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>D.</strong> Renal ultrasound showing bilateral hydronephrosis</p>

<details class="quiz-answer-block">
<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: A</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A. Correct:</strong> Reduced renal perfusion causes the kidneys to conserve sodium, so a low urine sodium supports a prerenal cause.</p>
<p><strong>B. Incorrect:</strong> Granular casts suggest tubular injury and an intrarenal cause.</p>
<p><strong>C. Incorrect:</strong> Marked retention points to lower urinary tract obstruction and a postrenal cause.</p>
<p><strong>D. Incorrect:</strong> Hydronephrosis indicates obstruction of urine flow and a postrenal cause.</p>
</div>
</details>

## Question 2

<p>A nurse is caring for a client who developed acute kidney injury after receiving an aminoglycoside antibiotic. Which type of kidney injury should the nurse suspect?</p>

<p class="answer-choice"><strong>A.</strong> Prerenal hypoperfusion</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>B.</strong> Intrarenal tubular injury</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>C.</strong> Postrenal outlet obstruction</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>D.</strong> Functional urinary retention</p>

<details class="quiz-answer-block">
<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: B</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A. Incorrect:</strong> This category results from reduced renal blood flow rather than direct nephron damage.</p>
<p><strong>B. Correct:</strong> This category is consistent with nephrotoxic injury to renal tubular cells.</p>
<p><strong>C. Incorrect:</strong> This category results from impaired urine drainage distal to the kidneys.</p>
<p><strong>D. Incorrect:</strong> This condition affects bladder emptying but does not explain nephrotoxic tubular damage.</p>
</div>
</details>
