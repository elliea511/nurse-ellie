# Immune & Inflammatory Disorders Supplemental Practice Quiz

<p class="quiz-subtitle">These questions cover rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, gout, systemic lupus erythematosus, anti-inflammatory and immunosuppressive medications, antineoplastic toxicities, and antimicrobial safety.</p>

## Question 1

<p>A nurse is comparing rheumatoid arthritis with osteoarthritis. Which finding is most characteristic of rheumatoid arthritis?</p>

<p class="answer-choice"><strong>A.</strong> Pain limited to one weight-bearing joint</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>B.</strong> Symmetric small-joint inflammation with prolonged morning stiffness</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>C.</strong> Joint pain that worsens throughout the day without systemic findings</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>D.</strong> Bony enlargement of distal finger joints</p>

<details class="quiz-answer-block">
<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: B</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A. Incorrect:</strong> This pattern is more consistent with a local degenerative condition.</p>
<p><strong>B. Correct:</strong> RA commonly produces symmetric inflammatory involvement and prolonged stiffness after rest.</p>
<p><strong>C. Incorrect:</strong> This pattern is more typical of osteoarthritis.</p>
<p><strong>D. Incorrect:</strong> Distal bony nodes are associated with osteoarthritis.</p>
</div>
</details>

## Question 3

<p>A client with rheumatoid arthritis has an acute flare. Which intervention should the nurse recommend?</p>

<p class="answer-choice"><strong>A.</strong> Balance rest with gentle activity and protect inflamed joints</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>B.</strong> Perform forceful range-of-motion exercises every hour</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>C.</strong> Apply continuous heat throughout the day</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>D.</strong> Remain on complete bed rest until all pain resolves</p>

<details class="quiz-answer-block">
<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: A</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A. Correct:</strong> Rest reduces inflammation while gentle movement limits stiffness and loss of function.</p>
<p><strong>B. Incorrect:</strong> Movement should not be forced through pain or resistance.</p>
<p><strong>C. Incorrect:</strong> Heat or ice should be time-limited to protect tissue.</p>
<p><strong>D. Incorrect:</strong> Prolonged immobility increases stiffness and deconditioning.</p>
</div>
</details>
