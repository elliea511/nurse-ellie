## Question 1

<p>A nurse is assessing a client who has systemic lupus erythematosus. Which finding most strongly suggests development of lupus nephritis?</p>

<p class="answer-choice"><strong>A.</strong> New 3+ proteinuria</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>B.</strong> Photosensitive facial rash</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>C.</strong> Morning joint stiffness</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>D.</strong> Episodes of Raynaud phenomenon</p>

<details class="quiz-answer-block">
<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: A</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A. Correct:</strong> Significant protein loss in the urine indicates glomerular involvement and possible lupus nephritis.</p>
<p><strong>B. Incorrect:</strong> This is a common cutaneous manifestation of lupus but does not specifically indicate renal damage.</p>
<p><strong>C. Incorrect:</strong> This reflects musculoskeletal inflammation rather than kidney involvement.</p>
<p><strong>D. Incorrect:</strong> This is a vascular manifestation of lupus and does not identify nephritis.</p>
</div>
</details>

## Question 3

<p>A client taking prednisone for an autoimmune disorder says, “I feel better, so I plan to stop it tomorrow.” Which response should the nurse make?</p>

<p class="answer-choice"><strong>B.</strong> “Take the entire daily dose every other day.”</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>C.</strong> “The medication should be tapered as prescribed.”</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>D.</strong> “Replace it with an over-the-counter anti-inflammatory.”</p>

<details class="quiz-answer-block">
<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: C</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
