## NCLEX Quick Cues: Immune & Inflammatory

<div class="priority-focus">
  <div>
    <h3>Think First</h3>
    Infection • Immunosuppression • CD4 Count • Viral Load • Joint Function • Organ Damage • CBC • Liver • Kidneys • Eyes
  </div>
</div>

<div class="detail-grid" markdown="1">

<div class="detail-mini-card" markdown="1">
<strong>HIV Cue</strong>

- **CD4** tells immune strength.
- **Viral load** tells how much virus is present.
- CD4 below **200 cells/mm³** is the major AIDS cue.
- Fever, dyspnea, neurologic changes, and vision changes are priority findings.
</div>

<div class="detail-mini-card" markdown="1">
<strong>Autoimmune Cue</strong>

- RA and lupus are systemic autoimmune disorders.
- RA threatens joint function and can progress to bony ankylosis.
- Lupus threatens kidneys, heart, lungs, blood cells, skin, and nervous system.
- New organ findings outrank chronic pain.
</div>

<div class="detail-mini-card" markdown="1">
<strong>Medication Cue</strong>

- Fever in an immunosuppressed client is urgent.
- Methotrexate means CBC and liver monitoring.
- Hydroxychloroquine means eye exams.
- Biologics mean TB screening and infection risk.
- Steroids are never stopped abruptly after long-term use.
</div>

</div>

<div class="detail-table">

| When You See This | Think This | Best Nursing Move |
|---|---|---|
| **CD4 below 200** | AIDS-level immune suppression | Prevent infection and assess for opportunistic disease |
| **Rising viral load** | Poor suppression, missed doses, or resistance | Assess adherence and report the trend |
| **Fever + cough + dyspnea in HIV** | Opportunistic respiratory infection | Assess airway, breathing, oxygenation, and notify provider |
| **Purple or brown lesions in advanced HIV** | Kaposi’s sarcoma | Assess and report new lesions |
| **Symmetric small-joint pain + prolonged morning stiffness** | Rheumatoid arthritis | Preserve function and control inflammation |
| **Local weight-bearing joint pain worse with use** | Osteoarthritis | Encourage joint protection, weight control, and low-impact activity |
| **Sudden red hot great toe** | Gout | Rest joint, give acute anti-inflammatory therapy, and reinforce fluids |
| **Butterfly rash + photosensitivity + joint pain** | Lupus | Protect from sun and assess renal and cardiac involvement |
| **Proteinuria + edema + hypertension in lupus** | Lupus nephritis | Monitor urine, BUN, creatinine, weight, and blood pressure |
| **Fever while taking methotrexate or a biologic** | Serious infection | Hold and report according to orders |
| **Mouth sores + bruising on methotrexate** | Bone-marrow suppression or toxicity | Check CBC and notify provider |
| **Visual changes on hydroxychloroquine** | Retinal toxicity | Stop activity and report promptly |
| **Tendon pain on a fluoroquinolone** | Tendinitis or rupture risk | Hold the dose and report |
| **Tinnitus + rising creatinine on gentamicin** | Ototoxicity and nephrotoxicity | Hold and report immediately |
| **Flushing during rapid vancomycin infusion** | Red man syndrome | Slow or stop infusion per protocol and notify provider |

</div>

<div class="mh-columns" markdown="1">

<div class="quick-review" markdown="1">
<h3>Priority Order</h3>

1. Airway and breathing with anaphylaxis, respiratory infection, or infusion reaction.
2. Infection in any immunosuppressed client.
3. Organ damage involving kidneys, heart, lungs, brain, or vision.
4. Bone-marrow, liver, kidney, or retinal medication toxicity.
5. Preserve joint function and mobility.
6. Reinforce long-term adherence and prevention teaching.
</div>

<div class="do-not-miss" markdown="1">
<h3>Fast Traps</h3>

- Do not use special isolation for HIV alone.
- Do not confuse CD4 count with viral load.
- Do not stop ART when the client feels better.
- Do not force ROM past pain or resistance.
- Do not ignore fever with a DMARD or biologic.
- Do not stop corticosteroids suddenly.
- Do not give tetracycline with milk, iron, zinc, or antacids.
- Do not infuse vancomycin rapidly.
</div>

</div>

<div class="detail-callout" markdown="1">
<strong>Immune NCLEX Rule:</strong> Infection and organ toxicity outrank chronic pain, and immunosuppressive medications always require monitoring.
</div>
