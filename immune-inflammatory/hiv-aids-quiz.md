# HIV/AIDS Additional Practice Questions

<p class="quiz-subtitle">These questions cover HIV immune function, HIV versus AIDS, transmission, testing, stages, antiretroviral therapy, opportunistic infections, cancers, wasting, neurologic changes, exposure response, nutrition, oral care, confidentiality, and patient education.</p>

## Question 1

<p>A nurse is teaching a client about the effect of HIV on the immune system. Which cell does HIV primarily target?</p>

<p class="answer-choice"><strong>A.</strong> CD4 T-helper lymphocyte</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>B.</strong> B lymphocyte</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>C.</strong> Neutrophil</p>
<p class="answer-choice"><strong>D.</strong> Platelet</p>

<details class="quiz-answer-block">
<summary>Click to see answer and rationale</summary>
<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: A</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
<p><strong>A. Correct:</strong> HIV primarily infects and destroys CD4 T-helper cells, weakening cell-mediated immunity.</p>
<p><strong>B. Incorrect:</strong> B cells produce antibodies but are not the primary cellular target of HIV.</p>
<p><strong>C. Incorrect:</strong> Neutrophils provide innate defense but are not the main target of HIV.</p>
<p><strong>D. Incorrect:</strong> Platelets support clotting and are not the primary immune cell affected.</p>
</div>
</details>

<p class="quiz-answer"><strong>Answer: B</strong></p>
<div class="quiz-rationale">
<p><strong>Rationale:</strong></p>
